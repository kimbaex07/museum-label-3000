## The line

> Parking Meter, c. 2025: citizens fed this iron box coins to rent the patch of ground beneath their car, then hurried back before its patience ran out.

## Why it works

- **On subject:** a museum label for an ordinary object from today — a parking meter.
- **Year-3000 misreading:** the future museum treats paying for parking as feeding coins to a hungry iron box to rent the ground itself, and treats the expiry timer as the box losing patience.
- **True and absurd:** drivers really did feed coins into a box, really did pay for the patch of ground under the car, and really did rush back before time expired.
- **Straight-faced label voice:** short, confident, no throat-clearing, no explanation of the joke — the humour is in the line itself.
- **Fresh angle:** not the obvious "tiny robot" or "coin-eating monster" gag; the joke is the rental of ground and the box's "patience".
